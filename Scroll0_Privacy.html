Privacy Policy for Scroll 0%

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Scroll 0% ("the App") is a minimalist home-screen launcher that helps you use your phone more intentionally. It is developed by Salman Padder (MSP Store) ("we", "us"). We designed the App to be private by default: it runs on your device, and we do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no user accounts.

Summary

In short: the App works entirely on your phone. Your settings and screen-time counters stay on your device, nothing is sent to our servers (we don't operate any), and the only payment handling is done by the Google Play Store if you choose to subscribe. You can remove everything the App stores at any time by clearing its data or uninstalling it.

Information We Collect

We collect nothing. The App has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. We do not operate a server or database that receives your information, and we do not share or sell data to anyone, because we never receive it in the first place.

Data Stored on Your Device

To do its job, the App saves some information locally on your phone. This never leaves your device and is not visible to us:

  • Your preferences and layout, such as folders, app organisation, toggles, and chosen colours.
  • Your screen-time settings and the daily usage counters that power optional time limits.
  • An optional password you may set to protect certain settings. This is stored only in a securely hashed (scrambled) form, never as readable text, and cannot be recovered by us.

All of this is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

App Usage Information (Screen-Time Features)

If you choose to turn on the optional Strict Screen Time feature, the App uses Android's Usage Access to see how long you spend in certain categories of apps (for example social media and entertainment) so it can count that time against the daily limit you set and gently guide you back to your home screen when the limit is reached. This information is read and used only on your device, in real time. It is not stored off-device, not transmitted, and not shared. This feature is entirely optional. If you don't enable it, the App does not use Usage Access at all.

Permissions We Request, and Why

The App requests only the permissions needed to power features you choose to use. Each one is used solely on your device:

PermissionWhat it's for
Vibrate Provides optional haptic (vibration) feedback when you interact with the launcher.
Set wallpaper Lets you apply a simple solid colour to your lock screen from within the App.
Do Not Disturb access
(notification policy)		 Powers the optional timed silent mode, so the App can turn Do Not Disturb on and off for the duration you choose.
Usage access Used only if you enable Strict Screen Time, to measure your app-usage time against the daily limits you set (see the section above).
Foreground service
(and special-use type)		 Keeps the screen-time timer running reliably while you use your apps, so your chosen daily limit is tracked accurately. It runs only while the feature is on, does no network activity, and shows a quiet ongoing notification so you always know it's active.
Show notifications Displays the quiet timer notification and the optional heads-up reminders as you approach a screen-time limit. You can disable notifications in your phone's settings.
View installed apps
(app queries)		 As a launcher, the App needs to list the apps on your phone so you can open and organise them. This list is used only to show your app menu and is not collected.

Optional permissions can be declined, or turned off later in your phone's settings. If a permission is off, the related feature simply stays off and the rest of the App keeps working.

Subscriptions and Payments

Scroll 0% Premium is an optional subscription handled entirely by the Google Play Store. We do not see, process, or store your payment details. Your subscription status is managed by Google and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

Opening Other Apps

As a launcher, the App's purpose is to open the other apps you've installed. Once you open another app, that app's own privacy policy and terms apply to what happens there. We are not responsible for the practices of other apps or services.

Data Sharing and Selling

We do not share, rent, or sell personal information to third parties. There are no advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or tracking libraries in the App.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data stays on your device, you are always in control of it. To delete everything the App has stored, clear the App's data in your phone's settings or uninstall the App. Since we don't receive your data, there is nothing stored on our side to request or delete.

Security

Keeping data on-device and off the network is itself a strong privacy protection. Any password you set is stored only as a salted, hashed value, not as readable text. No method of storage is ever perfectly secure, but we've aimed to keep the App's footprint minimal by design.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data a company holds about you. Because Scroll 0% keeps your data on your own device and we hold none of it, you can exercise these rights directly by managing or clearing the App's data on your phone. If you have any questions, you're welcome to contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add a new feature. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact Us
