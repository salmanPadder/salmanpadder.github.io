Privacy Policy for Digital Tasbee

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Digital Tasbee app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. Digital Tasbee is developed by Salman Padder (MSP Store). The App does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email, or contacts. Your counter data and settings stay on your device. The App displays advertisements provided by a third-party ad service, which may collect certain device-related information as described below.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data ourselves. Digital Tasbee does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information, and we do not run our own analytics or tracking. However, our third-party advertising provider may collect certain information automatically, as described in the "Advertising" section below.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves your settings locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. It includes:

  • Your counter values.
  • Your custom button labels.
  • The number of counters you choose to display.
  • Your haptic (vibration) feedback preference and intensity.

This information is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. If device backup is enabled in your Android settings, these preferences may be included in your personal device/cloud backup, which is controlled by you and your operating system, not by us.

Advertising

Digital Tasbee shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To show ads and measure their performance, AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as:

  • Your device's Advertising ID.
  • IP address and approximate (non-precise) location derived from it.
  • Device and operating system details (model, OS version, language, screen size).
  • App usage and ad interaction data (such as ad impressions and clicks).
  • Diagnostic and crash information related to ads.

This data is collected and processed by the ad provider under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your personal data from the ad provider, and we do not sell your information. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your Advertising Choices

  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android device settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • If you opt out of personalization, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to your interests.

Permissions

Digital Tasbee requests the following permissions:

  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE): used only to provide optional haptic feedback when you tap a button. It does not access any personal data.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET) and Network state (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used to load and display ads.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): allows the ad provider to use your device's Advertising ID for ad delivery and measurement. You can reset or disable it in your device settings.

Links to Other Services

The App may contain a link to its listing on the Google Play Store (for example, to let you rate the App), and ads may link to external websites or apps. If you follow such a link, you leave the App and the privacy policy of that external service applies. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external services or advertisers.

Children's Privacy

Digital Tasbee is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps. The App contains no in-app purchases.

Data Retention and Security

Data stored on your device remains there until you clear it or uninstall the App. Data collected by the advertising provider is retained according to that provider's own policies. We take reasonable steps to keep the App secure, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
