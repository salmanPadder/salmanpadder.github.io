Privacy Policy for Scan Wise

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Scan Wise mobile application (package com.mspstore.scanner, the “App”) handles information. We have tried to keep this short and accurate: the App is designed to work on your device, and we do not run servers that receive your documents.

Documents you scan

Documents, images and PDFs you create with the App are stored locally on your device — in the App’s private storage and, when you export, in the folder you choose. We (the developer) do not collect, upload, view, or transmit your scanned documents. They remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App, and any files you exported remain in the folder you selected.

Camera

The App requests camera access for one purpose: to let you photograph (scan) documents. Captured images are processed on your device and saved as described above. The camera is not used for any other purpose.

Information we do not collect

The App has no sign-in or account system. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location. (If you choose to email our support address, we will of course see the information you put in that email.)

Advertising

The App displays ads using Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain information, such as a device advertising identifier and general device and usage data, in accordance with Google’s policies. We do not control what Google collects. You can learn more here:

  • How Google uses information / advertising
  • Google AdMob & data

We request that only non‑adult ad content (up to a “Teen” maturity rating) be shown. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the App presents a consent choice before serving personalized ads, and you can change that choice later via Settings → Privacy options in the App.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: remove scans in the App, or uninstall the App to remove its local data.
  • Advertising identifier: you can reset or delete it in your device’s Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Consent: where applicable, update your ad‑consent choice from Settings → Privacy options in the App.

Children

Scan Wise is a general‑purpose document tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: support.mspstore@gmail.com.
